                📖 Game Rules, Tutorials & Strategic Guides
              

              
            


            

              

                
♟️ Chess (Player vs Minimax AI)

                
Standard FIDE rules. White moves first. Select a white piece to reveal green dots for valid moves. Depth 3 uses Alpha-Beta pruning to calculate hundreds of board trajectories in milliseconds.

              


              

                
🔴 Connect 4

                
Drop checkers into a 7-column gravity grid. First player to connect 4 of their color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins. Pro tip: Controlling the center column gives the most diagonal combinations!

              


              

                
🔢 2048

                
Use Arrow keys or WASD to slide tiles across the 4x4 grid. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one (2+2=4, 4+4=8... 1024+1024=2048). Strategy: Keep your highest value tile locked in a corner (e.g. bottom-right) and build monotonic descending chains!

              


              

                
🧩 Sudoku 9x9

                
Fill the 9x9 grid so every row, column, and 3x3 box contains digits 1 through 9 with zero duplicates. Click a cell to select it, then press 1-9 on your keyboard or keypad. Red highlights indicate conflicting duplicate digits.

              


              

                
🐍 Cyber Snake

                
Navigate the glowing cyber snake to consume energy nodes. Each node increases length and score. Avoid running into walls or the snake's own tail. Use smooth turns and coil along outer boundaries.

              


              

                
💣 Minesweeper

                
Numbers reveal how many adjacent mines are hidden in the 8 neighboring squares. Left click to reveal, Right click to flag a suspected mine. Look out for the classic 1-2-1 edge pattern!
